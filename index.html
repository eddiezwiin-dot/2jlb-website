01 — Corporate purpose

What we do

"Stewardship of land and property, built to outlast a single cycle."

2JLB is a private real-estate holding company (SCI) registered in Limoges. Its purpose is to acquire, develop, improve and manage land and buildings, and to grant leases and rights of use over those assets. The company favours a patient, long-horizon approach to asset management over short-term transactions.

Registered under APE code 68.20B — rental of land and other real estate properties — 2JLB's activity centres on holding and leasing real property for tenants and partners in and around Limoges.
